What is Talksy?

Talksy is a simple language app built around one idea:
If you want to speak a language, you should probably spend some time speaking it.
That's it.

Nothing more, nothing less.

Scoring

Talksy uses your iPhone's dictation facility to listen to your response and compare it with the expected answer. Generally, you need to score more than 85% to move on to the next exercise.

A couple of points

  • Listen to yourself. iPhone dictation is very good and can often determine the correct response even when your actual pronunciation isn't that good.

    Make sure to listen to yourself before moving on. Your ears are the best judge.

    Remember, you're doing this to learn, not just to pass!

  • Sometimes dictation gets it wrong. Occasionally, iPhone dictation gets a response completely wrong and nothing you do seems to make a difference.

    Don't be concerned if you can't achieve 100% all the time. If it sounds right, it probably is.

Lessons

Talksy includes lessons suitable for all ages. Hopefully, you'll find them amusing, maybe even useful. They're a bit different.

Vocabulary has been the driving force behind their creation. I leave the grammar to the professionals.

If you don't like the built-in lessons — or simply want to expand on them — it's easy to create your own. Check out the Customise menu option.
