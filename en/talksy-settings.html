Appearance

How Talksy looks — theme, colours, and the lesson map.

App ThemeTalksy

Uses Talksy's own colour palette by default. Choose System instead to follow your device's Light/Dark mode.

Button StyleImage CardAdvanced

Changes the visual style of buttons throughout the app.

Background ColorYellow

Sets a custom background colour. Only appears when App Theme is set to Talksy.

Navigation BackgroundDark GlossAdvanced

Backdrop image behind the 3D lesson map — Dark Gloss or Red/White Checkerboard.

Navigation SelectionPulsatingAdvanced

How the current lesson is highlighted on the map. Glow is the energy-efficient option and uses less battery.

Navigation Token TextOnAdvanced

Shows text labels on the lesson map's tokens. Turning this off can also save a little battery.

Show Welcome ScreenOn

Shows the welcome/onboarding screen when Talksy launches.

📚

Lesson Configuration

Scoring, playback, and what's shown on screen during a lesson.

Speaking Accuracy85%

The minimum match percentage your spoken answer needs to reach for a speaking exercise to be marked correct.

Speaking Accuracy (Vocab)50%

Same idea as Speaking Accuracy, but for vocabulary practice exercises.

Speaking Accuracy (Junior)50%Advanced

A separate, more forgiving accuracy threshold used specifically for younger learners.

Max Exercise Failures5

How many unsuccessful attempts are allowed on an exercise before the sad cat shows up and Talksy moves on.

Playback Rate50%Advanced

Adjusts the speed of spoken narration. Slower is easier — turn it down for beginners or tricky sounds.

Preferred VoicePrefer GenderAdvanced

Chooses which text-to-speech voice Talksy uses: matched to gender, matched to quality, or the device default.

Auto Play ExercisesOn

Automatically plays the audio for an exercise as soon as it opens.

Auto Playback RecordingsOn

Automatically plays back your own recording after you finish speaking.

Show Native TextOnAdvanced

Shows the text of an exercise in your native language.

Show Learning TextOnAdvanced

Shows the text of an exercise in the language you're learning.

Show Attempts CountOnAdvanced

Displays how many attempts you have left on the current exercise.

Allow Exercise SkippingOffAdvanced

Lets you skip past an exercise without completing it.

Allow Vocabulary SkippingOnAdvanced

Lets you skip past a vocabulary item without completing it.

🔁

Auto Play — Revision

Hands-free playback for reviewing a lesson passively, without speaking along.

Announce CourseOffAdvanced

Plays a short spoken introduction for the course before playback begins.

Announce LessonOnAdvanced

Plays a short spoken introduction for each lesson before it begins.

Translate ExerciseOn

Plays a translation of each exercise alongside the original.

Repeat SlowlyOn

Plays a slowed-down, word-by-word repetition of each exercise.

Repeat NormallyOff

Plays a normal-speed repetition of each exercise.

Beep Between ExercisesOffAdvanced

Plays a short beep between exercises — a useful audio cue if you're not looking at the screen.

Pause Breaks2sAdvanced

Length of the pause inserted between exercises during hands-free playback.

Repeat Count1Advanced

How many times each exercise is repeated during hands-free playback.

🎙️

Auto Play — Interactive

Hands-free mode where Talksy prompts you and waits for a spoken response.

Enable Interactive ModeOffAdvanced

Turns on interactive hands-free playback, where Talksy waits for your spoken response before continuing.

Translate ExerciseOnAdvanced

Plays a translation of each exercise before prompting you to respond.

Repeat SlowlyOnAdvanced

Plays a slowed-down repetition as part of the interactive prompt.

Repeat NormallyOffAdvanced

Plays a normal-speed repetition as part of the interactive prompt.

Failure PolicyRepeat SlowlyAdvanced

What Talksy does after a single unsuccessful attempt.

Max Failure PolicySkipAdvanced

What Talksy does once you've reached the maximum number of failed attempts.

Timeout15sAdvanced

How long Talksy waits for a spoken response before treating the attempt as missed.

📁

External Directories

For content authors working on custom course material — most learners won't need this.

External DirectoriesNoneAdvanced

Add folders on your device containing custom course content for Talksy to load, and remove them again when you're done.

🛠️

Debug

Internal tools used during development and content review.

Proof ModeOffAdvanced

A review aid used while proofreading lesson content — not needed for normal use of the app.
