What is Talksy?

Learn by speaking

Talksy is a simple language app built around one idea: if you want to speak a language, you should probably spend some time speaking it. So that's it.
Nothing more, nothing less.

🇬🇧 English 🇫🇷 French 🇩🇪 German 🇮🇹 Italian 🇪🇸 Spanish

Scoring

💯 Score yourself, then listen back

Talksy will give you a score, but your own ears are still the best judge. Listen to your recording before moving on — if it doesn't sound right, try again.

✍️ Write your own lessons

Preparing for an oral exam? Write your own script, practise your answers, and listen back to them on the bus, while walking the dog, or lying in bed.
